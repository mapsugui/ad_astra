Campaigns/tess-mono-01

Campaign · worlds.planetary.monotransit

tess-mono-01
TESS single-transit search

Search TESS SPOC light curves and cutouts for isolated transits, then audit each against detrending, blending, pointing and prior-art alternatives.

For TESS planet candidates known from a single transit (TOI table: no period, disposition PC or APC), screen their SPOC 120-s light curves for further isolated transit-like dips outside the known transit, with the screen's threshold calibrated on each light curve (sign-flip null) and its completeness measured by injection–recovery. A second dip would constrain the period; its absence is recorded as a bounded null with the measured sensitivity.
