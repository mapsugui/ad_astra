Campaigns/toi-121-01

Campaign · worlds.planetary.monotransit

toi-121-01
Known-object test, TOI-121.01

Known-object test on TOI-121.01: positive control on the catalogued transit(s), calibrated screen for further dips, catalogue cross-match. Runner: Calibration on each light curve (sign-flip null, injection–recovery): k* 3, ≤2.5, 3, 5, ≤2.5, ≤2.5; at the declared k = 5 the screen recovers ≥90% of 4-h dips only from depth 1%, 1%, 0.5%, 1%, 1%, 1% (completeness for 2000 ppm: 0%, 10%, 0%, 0%, 0%, 0%). The null result excludes only dips deeper than that. Positive control: catalogued transit gap (1 covered epoch). Residual screen: 297 threshold entries outside the known-signal veto across 6 light curve(s), forming 91 distinct event(s), 30 persistent in SAP and PDCSAP at two or more baselines.

Known-object test on TOI-121.01 (TIC 207081058, APC): check that the calibrated residual screen recovers the catalogued single transit in its retrieved light curve, measure the depth, then screen the same light curves for further transit-like dips outside the catalogued transits. A further dip is an unverified lead until vetted; it does not imply a period.
