tess-wasp12-residual-01
Residual screen of WASP-12, Sectors 20 and 43

Declared test: isolated negative excursions ≥5 robust-MAD, ≥2 cadences, in both SAP and PDCSAP, outside ±0.08 phase of the known transit.

In two verified SPOC 120-s light curves of TIC 86396382 / WASP-12 (Sectors 20 and 43), test whether any isolated negative excursion survives robust local-baseline subtraction, appears in SAP and PDCSAP flux, and lies outside the known WASP-12 b transit phase. A finite anomaly triage, not a novelty search.
