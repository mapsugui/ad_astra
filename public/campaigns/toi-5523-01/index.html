Campaign · worlds.planetary.monotransit

toi-5523-01
Known-object test, TOI-5523.01

Known-object test on TOI-5523.01: positive control on the catalogued transit(s), calibrated screen for further dips, catalogue cross-match. Runner: Calibration on each light curve (sign-flip null, injection–recovery): k* 3.5, 3, 4, 10, 4; at the declared k = 5 the screen recovers ≥90% of 4-h dips only from depth none on grid, none on grid, none on grid, none on grid, none on grid (completeness for 2000 ppm: 0%, 0%, 0%, 0%, 0%). The null result excludes only dips deeper than that. Positive control: catalogued transit partial (1 covered epoch). Residual screen: 3 threshold entries outside the known-signal veto across 5 light curve(s), forming 2 distinct event(s), 0 persistent in SAP and PDCSAP at two or more baselines.

Known-object test on TOI-5523.01 (TIC 443616612, PC): check that the calibrated residual screen recovers the catalogued single transit in its SPOC 120-s light curve, measure the depth, then screen the same light curves for further transit-like dips outside the catalogued transits. A further dip is an unverified lead until vetted; it does not imply a period.
