Gaia NSS vetting, TOI-2666.01

Gaia DR3 cone, SIMBAD cone and Gaia DR3 NSS two-body cross-match of the TOI-2666.01 host, as a discriminating test for the TOI-2666.01 sector-99 repeat-event lead (V-shaped events; ExoFOP note "v-shaped; possible EB"; campaigns/tess-mono-01/LEAD_VETTING_LOG.md). Runner: No light-curve product was retrieved; only image/table/text products are available to screen.

Gaia DR3 vetting of the TOI-2666.01 host (TIC 170889511) for the TOI-2666.01 sector-99 repeat-event lead (V-shaped events; ExoFOP note "v-shaped; possible EB"; campaigns/tess-mono-01/LEAD_VETTING_LOG.md). Question: does Gaia DR3 publish a non-single-star two-body solution for the host, or astrometric-quality and neighbour evidence, that bears on the eclipsing-binary / companion alternative? No light curve is analysed here.
