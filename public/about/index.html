Project Cygnus / Astraea · public repository

Research records from an astronomical data-forensics project

Cygnus re-examines public archive data for signals that survey pipelines may have discarded, and records every test that could show a signal is an artifact. This site publishes the parts of that work the operator has chosen to release.
