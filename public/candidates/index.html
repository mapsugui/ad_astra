Evidence levels

1
Unverified lead. A feature was observed; the artifact audit is incomplete.
2
Vetted candidate. Reproducible and survives every available artifact test; alternatives remain.
3
Independently supported. Corroborated by independent data, epochs, instruments or reductions.
4
Established. Confirmed, including by the community. Not reachable from this toolchain alone.

Any audit test not marked passed holds a record at level 1.

Audit states

  • passed
  • failed
  • inconclusive
  • not tested

A test that could not be run is “not tested”, never “passed”.
