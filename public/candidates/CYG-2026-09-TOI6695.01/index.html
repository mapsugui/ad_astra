CYG-2026-09-TOI6695.01

Two clean equal-depth ~3.5 ppt transits (reference S34, E1 S61) on TIC 118339710, 723.995 d apart; the S88 event E2 is rejected (difference-image centroid 12.0 arcsec off at 14.7 sigma, POS_CORR2 -5.2 sigma). 26 data-allowed aliases of dT = 723.995 d (P >= 27.8 d by the review reading; shortest density-compatible 20.69 d per event). No <=4-sigma secondary at the covered aliases, no capable neighbour within the blend radius; RV untested — Unverified lead.

Competing explanations

  • Transiting planet (P one of 26 aliases, possibly ~ 20-724 d; depth 3.5 ppt on a 1.56 Rsun host => Rp ~ 3.9 R_Earth).
  • Long-period grazing EB: allowed by two events; secondary hiding in seasonal gaps possible; RV is decisive.
  • Blend: disfavoured — no capable neighbour within the blend radius, difference image on-target at E1, empty common mode.
  • Sibling-planet transit: excluded by the sibling ephemerides (none predicted at the lead epoch).
