Repository/collections/tess-single-transit-01

Collection · campaign

TESS single-transit tests: 76 TOI targets and 2 Gaia NSS companion checks

The known-object test queue: 76 TOIs searched for isolated transits and period aliases, plus two Gaia DR3 NSS astrometric companion checks. Every campaign completed and was leaf-verified against its previous run; outcomes per item.

Published Completed · per-campaign outcomes · partly vetted

All 78 campaigns ran through the multi-archive runner (cygnus.multi) on this workstation (batch suite2-2026-09-26, 78/78 ok) and every committed record was verified leaf-by-leaf against the previous pass: no science value changed. Target positions are Gaia epoch J2015.5 (TOI-6663.01 at J2015.0), audited in reports/position-epoch-audit-01/. Lead-level campaigns carry per-lead vetting where it has run; evidence levels are copied verbatim from each record. Run logs live in the local ledger and batch journal (state/batches/suite2-2026-09-26/); TOI-2065.01 is a known VSX eclipsing binary (KELT, P = 71.96 d; SIMBAD classifies the host HD 111605 as a spectroscopic binary) and stays rejected.
