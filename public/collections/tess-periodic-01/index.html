TESS periodic-candidate screen, batch colab-p01 (100 of 1,000 targets)

First 100 of 1,000 queued periodic PC/APC TOIs processed end to end on a Google Colab CPU runtime (batch colab-p01, pinned commit 2b65d44). Outcomes: 2 unverified leads and 98 pipeline checks after lead vetting; 0 failed runs.

Published Completed runs · 2 unverified leads after vetting · 98 pipeline checks

Runs executed on Colab (CPU High-RAM, 8 vCPU) at --jobs 7, pinned to commit 2b65d44; the checkpoint shipped to Drive under Cygnus/colab_runs/colab-p01/ (3,330 objects) and was verified intact: all 3,327 manifest-listed files hash-match, all 100 specs byte-match the committed specs, and all 387 product SHA-256s inside the records match the Drive files.

Lead vetting 2026-09-26 (complete). Every lead in this batch was vetted (python -m cygnus.campaign vet, per-campaign vetting/ artifacts and per-lead readings in campaigns/tess-periodic-01/LEAD_VETTING_LOG.md). 51 of the 57 flagged repeat trains turned out to be the targets’ own catalogued ephemeris transits — the queue CSV carried no period column, so the specs ran with period_days: null while the TOI table already held the periods; the vet’s sibling-ephemerides check caught every one. Those 51 records are reconciled to pipeline_check. Two leads survive vetting with complete audits: TOI-224.01 (four on-target events fitting a single data-allowed period P = 31.5798 d; the TOI table’s own P 705.58 d is incommensurate with the observed train) and TOI-7610.01 (an event 28.7 catalogued periods from the reference, on-target difference image; a +5.9-sigma centroid excursion is the residual caveat). Both remain Unverified leads; no candidate dossier is published. Catalogue guards flagged: VSX collisions (toi-6806-01, toi-3157-01, toi-3241-01, toi-621-01), a SIMBAD EB* match (toi-630-01, 0.1 arcsec), a moving-object failure (toi-2782-01), positive-control failures (toi-340-01, toi-1149-01, toi-1986-01, toi-3157-01, toi-6568-01), and 8 no-data campaigns. The per-event pointing/quality census failed in 85/100 records — a systematic check-health finding under investigation, not 85 science signals.

Provenance limits, stated honestly. The run-level ledger for this batch is a companion Colab ledger (archived beside the batch; SHA-256 recorded in docs/STATUS.md), which is never merged into the local ledger, so these runs have no rows on the site log page; the sites log mechanism keys on exact script names and does not fit batch runs. Runner products stay on Drive, indexed in storage/locations.jsonl (colab_runs/colab-p01). The remaining 900 queued targets have specs and draft records but have not been run yet.
