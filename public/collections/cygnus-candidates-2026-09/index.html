CYG-2026-09-TOI224.01 — TOI-224.01 (TIC given in the record)

Download available Project-authored candidate

Four deep, equal-depth, equally-shaped dips measured on the target in four separate TESS sectors form a single harmonic family with P = 31.5798 d (n = 23/58/81/91 from the reference): one period fits every observed event spacing, and the TOI table's own P = 705.5845 d is incommensurate with the observed train (705.58/31.58 = 22.34, non-integer). A grazing companion (Rp/R* = 0.28) on an M dwarf is favoured over a blended EB (no capable Gaia neighbour, on-target difference images in E1-E3); the period and the planet-vs-EB question are unresolved (RV not tested) — Unverified lead.

CYG-2026-09-TOI2666.01 — TOI-2666.01 (TIC given in the record)

Download available Project-authored candidate

Two deep, coincidentally-equal V-shaped dips on HD 80133 measured in S35 and S99, 1790.006 d apart, survive every artifact test run (on-target difference image 0.4 arcsec/0.7 sigma, shape ratio 1.02 +/- 0.02, 158-sigma red-noise significance, no capable neighbour). All short SPOC-DV periods (13.93/34.37/7.50 d) and the P 36.53 d alias are refuted as two-event artifacts and parity-inconsistent rows; 52 data-allowed aliases (P >= 12.79 d) remain. A grazing planet (Rp ~ 1.1-1.4 Rjup) or an equal-depth grazing EB is unresolved — RV is decisive — Unverified lead.

CYG-2026-09-TOI3500.02 — TOI-3500.02 (TIC given in the record)

Download available Project-authored candidate

Two clean on-target transits on TIC 443666343 (the S90 event E1 and the S64 reference; the S101 event E2 is an aperture-loss/pointing artifact, rejected): equal depth ~7.0-7.8 ppt, equal 7.9-h duration and shape, 36-47-sigma red-noise significance, difference images 0.3-0.4 arcsec on target, the 3.71-arcsec co-moving neighbour excluded by the deficit direction. Sixteen data-allowed aliases P = 700.6242/n (n = 2..18 excluding 18.44 d; P >= 35.03 d) — P = 350.312 d is the most economical single reading. RV and phase coverage untested — Unverified lead.
