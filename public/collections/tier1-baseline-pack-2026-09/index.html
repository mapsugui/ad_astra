Tier-1 archive baseline pack, September 2026

Provenance record of a bounded test retrieval from eight anonymous-access archives (MAST, Gaia, SkyView, CDS, NED, ESO, IRSA, Legacy Surveys) around benchmark fields and well-known targets. Engineering baseline for access and provenance tooling; no scientific analysis has been run on it.

Published Engineering baseline · not a scientific result

The pack exercised each archive client and the provenance ledger: every product was checksummed and logged, and uploaded products were verified against their manifest before the local staging copy was removed.

What is published here is the manifest record: product IDs, checksums, retrieval times, endpoints, queries verbatim, recorded terms, and the outcome of every attempt, including exclusions and failures. The archive products themselves are not redistributed; follow the archive links to obtain them under each archive's terms.

Manifests were copied from the staging area into the worktree at the snapshot time shown for each pack; later changes to the staging area are not reflected until the next snapshot. The mast pack is an earlier staging run (TESScut cutouts, plus SPOC name probes that returned zero rows); 01_mast repeated the SPOC search with cone queries.

The earlier mast staging run's zero-row SPOC probes (2026-09-23) were transient and are superseded: the corrected 01_mast run found those targets, and the earlier manifest is listed as restricted rather than re-published.
