Positive control failed with the epoch covered (sector 25, 234 usable in-transit cadences; row updated 2022-11-18). Two readings, both from the runner's outputs:

  • Sensitivity first: at sector 25's k* = 3 the 90 %-completeness depth is 5000 ppm for every duration, and the catalogue transit is 2143 ppm over 8.06 h — below the screen's floor even if present, so a missed control is expected before any "wrong epoch" inference was needed.
