Reviewer notes (2026-09-25)#

Positive control failed formally at the covered sector-69 epoch (139 usable cadences; QUALITY=0, max gap 2 min): measured 270 ± 93 ppm vs catalogue 739 ppm (ratio 0.37). At k* = 2 the sector's 90%-completeness depth is 2000 ppm at 4 h; the 4.62-h catalogue signal is below calibrated sensitivity, and injection completeness for the reference 2000-ppm/4-h box is only 10%. Record the formal failure without changing thresholds or assigning a wrong epoch.

The three persistent rows are only 2–3 cadences: S68 at BJD 2460165.34592 and 2460168.50914, and S69 at 2460192.78131. Their ±0.15-d binned profiles show broad low-level depressed baselines and scatter, not isolated U-shaped events; QUALITY=0, gaps 2 min, and centroid residuals are within about 1σ. No repeat candidate was built because the positive control failed.

This TIC sits in the known multi-planet TOI-125 system: the archive query returned TOI-125 b/c/d, while TESS_TOI returned four TOI rows; VSX also returned an EP variable with P=4.65382 d within 30 arcsec. These matches warrant checking the event against known system ephemerides and verifying the source association. Do not describe this as a new object. Pixel/pointing and alternate-reduction tests remain not tested.
