Reviewer notes (2026-09-25)#

Positive control passed: the sector-98 profile (sector98/normalized_series.csv) shows a clean flat-bottomed dip centred on the catalogued epoch (BJD 2461037.4553), full width ≈ 8 h in both SAP and PDCSAP. The recovered depth (7511 ± 145 ppm) is deeper than the catalogue's 4244 ppm — read as dilution/detrending of the catalogue value, not a different event. No repeat candidate was raised (period_aliases not_tested; no persistent event matches the catalogued depth), so no escalation.

The two persistent screen events (2461003.31247 and 2461003.35275, sector 98) are one contiguous feature, not two transits: their mid-times are 0.040 d apart and each binned profile is a broad (≈ ±0.15 d), shallow (~3000–4000 ppm) depression that drifts rather than showing a flat floor. SAP and PDCSAP agree in shape. The nearest-excursion flux centroid is essentially unmoved — MOM_CENTR1 +0.0016/−0.0001 px and MOM_CENTR2 −0.0001/−0.0001 px against local scatter sd 0.0049/0.0095 px — so no pointing excursion accompanies the dip. It sits ≈ 34 d before the catalogued epoch, well outside the alias window. Read as a low-frequency systematic; at most an unverified lead, and not advanced.

Catalogue cross-match answered by all four services (SIMBAD returned TOI-941.01 and the host star); no service-outage caveat applies.
