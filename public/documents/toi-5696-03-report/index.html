Escalation (2026-09-25)#

Positive control failed on data that covers the epoch. Sector 72 (tess2023315124025-s0072-...) contains the catalogued epoch (BJD 2460284.7141) with 242 usable in-transit cadences, yet the screen recovered only 820 ± 48 ppm against a catalogue depth of 1204 ppm — not_recovered. docs/AGENT_RUNBOOK.md makes a positive-control failure on covered data an escalation. Per that rule, no threshold was tuned and no state was changed; the failure is reported as-is.

Most likely cause, from the runner's own calibration: at sector 72's k* = 3 the 90 %-completeness depth is 5000 ppm for an 8 h signal, and the catalogued transit is 1204 ppm over 8.09 h — about four times below the detection floor. A shallow-transit-below-sensitivity failure is therefore the first hypothesis, ahead of a wrong epoch or a wrong TIC. It cannot be distinguished further without an independent reduction or a deeper light curve, neither of which is built here.

Two further notes for a reviewer: the TIC is a multi-signal host (TESS_TOI lists TOI-5696.01 PC, .02 CP and .03 PC on the same TIC), so a blend with a neighbouring signal is a live alternative; and the three persistent events all lie in sector 46, which does not cover the epoch.

Reviewer notes (2026-09-25)#

Positive control failed on data that covers the epoch, so this is escalated above. Sector 72 contains the catalogued epoch (BJD 2460284.7141) with 242 usable in-transit cadences, but the screen found only 820 ± 48 ppm where the catalogue gives 1204 ppm — not_recovered. The other two retrieved light curves (sectors 45, 46) do not cover the epoch.

The sensitivity table gives the most likely explanation: at sector 72's calibrated threshold (k* = 3) the 90 %-completeness depth is 5000 ppm for an 8 h signal, and the catalogued transit is 1204 ppm over 8.09 h — roughly four times shallower than the detection floor. So the failure is consistent with the signal being below sensitivity rather than with a wrong epoch; it is recorded, not tuned around.

The three persistent screen events (2459563.12910 / .16521 / .21800, sector 46) span ≈ 0.09 d, i.e. one contiguous ~0.25–0.27 % feature, not three transits. Sector 46 does not cover the catalogued epoch. Catalogue cross-match answered by all four services: TESS_TOI lists TOI-5696.01 (PC), .02 (CP) and .03 (PC) on this TIC — a multi-signal TIC where the other dispositions matter for any interpretation.
