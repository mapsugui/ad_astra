Reviewer notes (2026-09-25)#

Positive control is inconclusive/partial at BJD 2458507.5198 (sector 7): 453 cadences, depth 152 ± 23 ppm vs catalogue 184 ppm, nearest-entry offset +3.86 h. The catalogued duration is 15.11 h, while the 90%-completeness table reaches only 2000 ppm at 8 h; no useful catalogue-depth null is available.

The six persistent entries group into three short/soft complexes: S7 at 2458491.72648/.73204/.73620 and 2458492.22647 (all 2 cadences), S1 at 2458335.24586, and S5 at 2458441.09061. The S7 binned profiles show a broad ~0.5–1.0 ppt soft depression with no distinct ingress/egress; QUALITY=0, gaps 2 min, centroid residuals within ~1σ. The S1/S5 entries are also two-cadence features; S1 has a 16-min max gap, and S5's row centroid is ~2σ but only two cadences, so that check is inconclusive. None is a repeat candidate. A separate large S4 SAP-only complex (36–84 cadences, absent in PDCSAP) is a prominent pipeline/systematics concern. No VSX variable match; pixel-level and independent-reduction audits remain untested.
