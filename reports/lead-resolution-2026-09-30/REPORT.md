## Photometric stress tests and finite nulls

`resolve_leads.py` jointly fits an additive linear or quadratic baseline and a fixed-duration box in a ±0.75 d local window. QUALITY must equal zero, both flux channels must be finite, and at least 80% of the expected event exposure plus adequate flanks must be present. It does not interpolate flux across gaps. Each of SAP and PDCSAP is tested under both baselines, yielding 48 product/channel/baseline combinations over 12 products. Each draws 400 out-of-event centres without replacement, rejects poorly covered windows, and preserves the original ordering/correlation within each fitted window. This is a local empirical null sampled from observed windows, not a complete block-resampled original-search false-alarm calculation.

At the eight accepted reference/repeat windows, all 32 channel/baseline event measurements have zero null exceedances among their own 377–399 valid null windows. These overlapping windows and channels are dependent and must not be pooled into a probability. Zero exceedances gives finite resolution of order 1/N; it is not zero probability or Gaussian significance. All 4,800 fixed-epoch box injections across the 48 combinations recovered at the adopted 76,000 / 15,500 / 7,000 ppm depths under the declared threshold. This establishes sensitivity for these injected fixed-duration boxes, not blind-search completeness for grazing or eccentric eclipses.

The old TOI-224 timing family, with S106 excluded, gives P=31.57966184 d for prescribed cycle numbers [0,23,58,81]. Residuals are −3.72, +2.46, +6.91 and −5.64 minutes. These are legacy box timestamps, and S69 remains localization-inconclusive. This is a conditional cycle fit without a timing posterior or uniqueness proof. It predicts cycle 92 at JD 2461271.175223, where the held-out S107 cutout contains the repeat. The fitted box centre differs by roughly 15 minutes; fixed duration, eclipse morphology and CCD-time correction prevent an astrophysical TTV interpretation.

The S107 event survives radius-1.5 and radius-2 apertures with a frame-by-frame outer-pixel median background and both baselines. Each variant recovers 100/100 fixed-window injections. The local grid and exposure coverage are recorded, including the rejected/gap windows. A quadratic per-pixel baseline plus box produces the deficit map; 300 length-five cadence residual-block resamples give a 0.618″ displacement at a 4.71 bootstrap ratio. Calibration/WCS covariance and the 0.061″ close pair are unresolved. **Localization is inconclusive**, despite a small angular displacement. The outer-background box shows a brightening (~−1,523 ppm), not a comparable deep dip. Same-CCD controls, full pointing telemetry and moving-object checks for this new event remain not tested.

S8 TOI-2666 has 30-minute exposures, so a 1.16-hour event spans only a few samples. The added finite-exposure box test considers both 1.156/1.445-hour widths, both apertures/baselines, and ±0.05 d timing slack around each predicted alias. Sixteen of 52 baseline aliases predict a S8 window. **None is excluded across all trials.** Some local fits show large excursions; QUALITY==0 alone does not make these reductions adequate. No new transit is promoted from this coarse extraction, no aliases are removed, and no clean nondetection is claimed.

Conversions throughout: **1 ppt = 1,000 ppm = 0.1%**. Thus a 76,000-ppm event is 76 ppt = 7.6%, not 7.6 ppt. No new companion radius is quoted because the host/component/dilution priors are unresolved.

## Calibrated PRF attempt and falsification limits

The official UPDATED_2.0 PRFs are bilinearly interpolated between four bracketing detector locations for each camera/CCD, and sampled on the published 9×9 subpixel grid. TPF physical WCS and PRF physical WCS share the raw column convention; the documented 44-column collateral offset is not applied twice. Gaia J2016.0 proper motions are propagated to each observation. An out-of-event two-source plus planar-background fit profiles common registration from −0.2 to +0.2 pixel in steps of 0.02. Per-pixel model floors of 1%, 3% and 5% of control flux are declared sensitivity choices, not empirical calibration covariance.

At S64, accepted registrations favor the neighbour at 1–3% floors; the 5% range includes both signs. At S90, the 1% subset favors the target, while the 3–5% subsets include both signs. Absolute difference-fit preferences are weak under these conservative floors, and the static control-fit residuals are too large for a confident calibrated localization.
